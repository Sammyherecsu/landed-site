Common questions

More detail lives in our Help Center.

Do I need a dollar card to use Glavand?

No. You pay in naira with a Nigerian card, bank transfer or USSD, and we handle the dollar side. This applies to shopping, gifts and international payments alike.

Which US stores can I buy from?

Any legitimate American retailer. Amazon, Walmart, Target, eBay, Best Buy, Apple, Nike, Sephora, Ulta, Back Market and SHEIN are the ones people use most, but our calculator accepts a link from any store.

How long does delivery to Nigeria take?

Ten to fourteen days by air from the moment your payment clears, with two to four extra days for delivery outside Lagos. Gifts sent within the United States arrive in three to five days.

Is customs duty included in the price?

Yes. Duty is estimated and built into your total on shipped orders, so there is nothing extra to pay when your package arrives. Gifts delivered inside the United States do not attract Nigerian customs duty at all.

What kind of fees can you pay for me?

University tuition and application fees, WES and other credential evaluations, SEVIS, GRE, IELTS, TOEFL, professional body dues, visa and immigration portals, conference registration, flights and hotels. If you are unsure, send us the payment page and we will confirm.

How do I know my money is safe?

Payments run through Paystack, so we never see or store your card details. We only pay verified institutions, recognised merchants and official portals, never a personal account. If we cannot verify where the money is going, we refund you in full.

Where in Nigeria do you deliver?

Nationwide. You can collect from a partner branch in your state, which is the cheaper option, or have your order delivered to your address.
