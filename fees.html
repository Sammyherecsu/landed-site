Straight answers

Do I need a dollar card or domiciliary account?

No. That's the whole point. You pay us in naira and we settle them in dollars.

How long does it take?

We pay of your payment clearing, usually much sooner. If your deadline is tight, message us on WhatsApp and we'll prioritise it.

What proof do I get?

Whatever they issue: a payment receipt, a confirmation email, or a portal screenshot showing your reference marked paid. We send it to you as soon as we have it.

Will you need my portal login?

Usually not. Most institutions let us pay directly with your reference number. Some portals only allow payment once signed in. In those cases you can either complete the payment yourself with funds we provide, or share access and we'll handle it. We use it only for that payment and delete it afterwards.

What if a flight fare or hotel rate changes before you pay?

Fares move and rooms sell out. We check the price before paying. If it has gone up we message you with the new total and only proceed once you agree. If it has dropped, you pay less. If it has gone entirely, you get a full refund.

What if the payment fails or the portal rejects it?

You get back the exact naira amount you paid, with nothing deducted. If it failed because the reference number was wrong, we'll ask you for the correct one and retry, unless you'd rather have the money back.

Can you guarantee my admission, visa or exam result?

No, and be wary of anyone who says otherwise. We move the money and give you the receipt. Everything after that is between you and them.

What fees can't you pay?

Anything going to a personal account, an unverifiable merchant, or a portal we can't independently confirm as official. If we can't verify it, we refund you in full rather than guess.

Why is your rate different from the official rate?

Our rate includes the cost of sourcing dollars and moving them internationally. It's shown at the top of this page and in your total before you pay, with nothing added afterwards.
